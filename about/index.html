About BusinessCalc.ca

BusinessCalc.ca was created to help small business owners make smarter decisions with simple, practical and free calculators.

Running a business means making money decisions constantly: what to charge, whether a product really earns, when a hire pays for itself, how much tax sits inside a receipt. The arithmetic behind those decisions is not hard, but it is scattered, and in the moment it often gets skipped or guessed.

BusinessCalc.ca puts the most common calculations in one place, written in plain English. Every calculator shows its formula, walks through a worked example, and explains what the result means, because a number you understand is a number you can act on. Whether you are pricing your first product, checking an Etsy listing after fees, planning your freelance rate or preparing to hire, the goal is the same: business decisions based on numbers, not guesswork.

What You Will Find Here

Thirteen focused calculators covering profit and revenue, e-commerce profit for Shopify and Etsy sellers, employee and freelance costs, Canadian sales taxes, pricing, break-even and business loans, plus a growing library of guides in the Resources section that explain the thinking behind each tool.

What BusinessCalc.ca Is Not

It is not financial, tax, accounting or legal advice, and it does not replace a qualified professional. Results are estimates for education and planning. Read our Disclaimer for the details, and see the Privacy Policy for how the site handles information: every calculation runs in your own browser.

Questions or Suggestions?

If a calculator would help your business, or you spot something that looks wrong, we want to hear about it through the Contact page.
