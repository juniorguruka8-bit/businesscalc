Frequently Asked Questions

Are the BusinessCalc.ca calculators really free?

Yes. Every calculator on BusinessCalc.ca is free to use, with no account, no sign-up and no usage limit. The site exists to help small business owners make clearer decisions about pricing, profit, tax, hiring and growth.

Do I need to create an account?

No. BusinessCalc.ca calculators work instantly in your browser. There is nothing to install and nothing to log in to.

Are the results financial advice?

No. Results are estimates for educational and planning purposes. They help you understand your numbers, but important financial, tax, accounting or legal decisions should be verified with a qualified professional.

Which calculator should I start with?

If you sell products, start with the Profit Calculator or the Shopify Profit Calculator. If you set prices, use the Pricing Calculator. If you hire or freelance, the Employee Cost and Hourly Rate calculators are the most useful starting points.

Can I use BusinessCalc.ca for my Canadian business?

Yes. Several tools are built with Canadian businesses in mind, including the Sales Tax Calculator with GST, HST, PST, RST and QST rates by province. Verify current tax rates with the appropriate government authority before filing.
